# Strategic Positioning

## Category bet

PDM is **not** "another Spotify." The stronger category is a **music-first fandom
platform** where listening, exclusive content, community, and direct fan support
live around the artist.

> Spotify owns passive listening. Instagram owns noisy attention. YouTube owns
> generic video distribution. **PDM should own music fandom.**

Emotional promise: *"Don't just listen to an artist. Be near their career."*

## Product thesis

More than streaming, but **music stays the center of gravity**. Users can listen,
watch backstage/release videos, read artist posts, comment, join fan chats, and
support artists — all without leaving the music experience. See
[[surfaces-overview]] and [[persistent-music-layer]].

## Differentiation

- **vs Spotify** — Spotify is catalog + playlists + recommendation, shallow on
  artist–fan relationships and direct monetization. PDM wins on context, access,
  community, support, and the artist-owned relationship — **not** catalog scale.
- **vs YouTube** — too broad; music competes with unrelated content. PDM is
  music-native: video exists because it relates to an artist/release/fan base.
- **vs Instagram / TikTok** — generic social attention with too much noise. PDM
  keeps normal users mostly in comments, reactions, chats, and votes — **not** in
  public posting of random content.

## Product rule

Do **not** build a general-purpose social feed. Organize content around artists,
tracks, releases, posts, galleries, videos, fan communities, direct support, and
future funding. The main feed is **music-native**, not random-user-native. This
rule drives [[specific-content-tables]] and the [[artist-page]] design.

## Target wedge

- **Users**: core/super fans, fans of local scenes and emerging artists, people
  who already buy merch/tickets and follow creative processes.
- **Artists**: emerging independent and local-scene artists with ~500–10,000 real
  followers elsewhere (Telegram, Discord, IG, TikTok, YouTube) who need a home
  base for monetization and fan relationships. Not global superstars, not
  cold-start.

Strategic position: *PDM is a home base for independent artists to convert
listeners into fans, fans into supporters, and supporters into participants in the
artist's growth.* Spotify/YouTube remain discovery channels; PDM is where the
relationship deepens.

## Key product risk

- Become a generic social platform → lose the edge.
- Become only streaming → lose to Spotify.
- Become only Patreon-for-musicians → music stops being central.

Winning position = the combination: **streaming + artist context + fandom +
direct support + future participation.** The phased path is in [[roadmap-phases]];
the funding layer is deliberately sequenced last — see [[defer-investment-layer]].
