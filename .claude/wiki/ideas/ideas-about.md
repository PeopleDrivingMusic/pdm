# Ideas

A holding bucket for interesting, **not-yet-decided** sparks worth keeping —
product, technical, or monetization. Lower bar than the rest of the wiki: capture
first, refine later.

Conventions:
- One idea per page, `kebab-case.md`, `type: idea`.
- Link to related concept/strategy/decision pages (see [[index]] to find them).
- When an idea is adopted, promote it into a concept/strategy/decision page and mark
  the idea `status: superseded` with a link to where it landed.

No ideas captured yet. Add them here as they come up (see `WIKI.md` → Ingest).
