# Fan Features

What a paying fan unlocks. Per-artist perks come with an [[artist-subscription]]
($1/mo); spending more adds app-wide [[loyalty-tiers]].

Per-artist (subscriber) perks:
1. **Exclusive content** — private posts, videos, demos, polls.
2. **Commenting** — paid subscribers only (spam protection).
3. **Offline mode** — caching the subscribed artist's tracks.
4. **Presales** — priority access to tickets and merch.
5. **No ads** — on the paid artist's tracks.

App-wide (loyalty) perks:
6. **Loyalty tiers** — $5/mo removes ads everywhere; $10/mo adds global merch/event
   discounts ([[loyalty-tiers]]).

Investor surface:
7. **Producer cabinet** — track returns from owned track shares and artist growth
   ([[revenue-share-crowdfunding]]).

These map to entitlements in the finance domain ([[subscription-and-purchase]]) and
to visibility levels on content (`public | followers | subscribers | investors`,
see [[post]]).
