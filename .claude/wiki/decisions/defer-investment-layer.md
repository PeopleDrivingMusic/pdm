# Decision — Defer the investment/funding layer

**Context.** Revenue-share crowdfunding ([[revenue-share-crowdfunding]]) is a major
differentiator but carries securities, KYC/AML, rights-accounting, fraud, and
compliance risk.

**Decision.** Ship it **last**, behind a proper legal structure or partner, after the
music/fandom/support layers are solid. Public promises don't lead with funding until
the legal structure is ready. Rollout order in [[roadmap-phases]]:
streaming → exclusive content → community → subscriptions → CRM/analytics → perks →
regulated crowdfunding.

**Alternatives.** Lead with investment as the headline feature (maximizes
differentiation but maximizes legal/compliance exposure and could make music feel
secondary).

**Consequences.** Lower early legal risk; music stays central ([[positioning]]). The
data model and [[economic-model]] still account for funding state now
([[subscription-and-purchase]]), so the later layer slots in. Artist eligibility is
gated by [[artist-grades]] and [[trustscore]].
