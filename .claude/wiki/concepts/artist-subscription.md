# Artist Subscription ($1/month)

The atomic unit of PDM monetization. A user subscribes to a **specific artist**
(not the platform) for **$1/month**, split **80% artist / 20% platform**.

Subscribing unlocks per-artist perks ([[fan-features]]):
- exclusive content (private posts, videos, demos, polls);
- the artist's fan chat;
- offline caching of that artist's tracks;
- no ads on that artist;
- presale access to tickets/merch;
- commenting (paid-only, for spam protection).

The subscription pool funds royalties via [[user-centric-royalties]]. Spending
beyond a single subscription unlocks app-wide [[loyalty-tiers]]. Contrast with the
later, heavier [[revenue-share-crowdfunding]] mechanic. See [[economic-model]] for
the full money flow and [[subscription-and-purchase]] for the data shape.
