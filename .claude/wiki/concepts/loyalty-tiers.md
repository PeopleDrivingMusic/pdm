# Loyalty Tiers

Spending beyond a single [[artist-subscription]] unlocks **platform-wide** perks
(in addition to the per-artist perks in [[fan-features]]):

- **from $5/month** — removes ads across the **entire** app (not just one artist);
- **from $10/month** — global discounts on merch and events.

Loyalty tiers reward total fan spend, reinforcing [[fan-base-monetization]]: the
more a fan supports, the more cross-artist value they receive. Tier identity also
feeds future early-support badges/perks (see [[roadmap-phases]] product step 6).
Part of the [[economic-model]].
