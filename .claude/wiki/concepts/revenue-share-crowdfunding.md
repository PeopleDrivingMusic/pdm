# Revenue-Share Crowdfunding (phase 2)

A second-phase mechanic: an artist offers a **share of a track's future
royalties** (e.g. 20%) via crowdfunding. Fans buy shares with fiat/crypto and earn
a percentage of that track's revenue — **inside PDM and on external platforms**
(Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube), where PDM acts as aggregator-licensee.

Lifecycle: artist opens a raise → fans buy shares (e.g. 0.1% for $5) → track
releases → revenue accrues → holders see returns in the **Producer cabinet** and
withdraw in crypto. If the artist signs to a major label, fans can take an
**"Exit"** (shares bought out at a multiplier) — the [[economic-model]] incubator
role.

Gating and risk:
- only artists who reach the **Creator** grade can launch a raise ([[artist-grades]]);
- [[trustscore]] caps crowdfunding limits for dirty traffic;
- carries securities, KYC/AML, rights-accounting, fraud, and compliance risk, so it
  ships last and behind a legal structure — see [[defer-investment-layer]] and
  [[roadmap-phases]].

Financially it lives in the strongly-consistent, auditable ledger domain
([[system-design]] §Payments, [[subscription-and-purchase]]).
