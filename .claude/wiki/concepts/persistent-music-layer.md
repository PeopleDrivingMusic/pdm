# Persistent Music Layer

A central UX principle: **music keeps playing while the user explores** comments,
track context, backstage, posts, fan chat, merch, and support/funding. The player
feels persistent and central — music is the gravity well that everything else
orbits.

This is what keeps PDM **music-first** even as social and commerce surfaces grow
([[positioning]]). It shapes the app shell (the `(app)` layout hosts a persistent
player and sidebar; see [[design-system]] and [[surfaces-overview]]) and reinforces
the [[artist-page]] as a place you *stay* rather than bounce through.
