# Fan-Base Monetization (the key shift)

PDM's founding thesis: move from **"pay per number of plays"** to **"build and
monetize a loyal fan base."** Revenue is driven by depth of relationship
(subscriptions, support, future ownership) rather than raw stream volume.

Consequences across the product:
- monetization is per-artist ([[artist-subscription]]), not per-stream;
- royalties follow the individual fan ([[user-centric-royalties]]);
- the product optimizes for converting *listeners → fans → supporters →
  participants* ([[positioning]]);
- success metrics emphasize retention, conversion, and loyalty over play counts.

This is the connective idea behind the [[economic-model]] and the
[[revenue-share-crowdfunding]] phase.
