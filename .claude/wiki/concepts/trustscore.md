# TrustScore (bot protection)

A two-sided reputation system that makes running AI/bot farms economically
unprofitable — critical because money follows listening ([[user-centric-royalties]]).

- **User TrustScore** — evaluates listener behavior patterns. Bots get minimal
  "weight," so their streams barely affect money distribution.
- **Artist TrustScore** — evaluates traffic cleanliness. Manipulation reduces
  crowdfunding limits and disables recommendations.

**Goal:** make maintaining bot farms cost more than they can extract.

TrustScore gates and weights several systems: royalty distribution, recommendations,
and [[revenue-share-crowdfunding]] limits. It complements [[artist-grades]] (which
gate *what* an artist can do) by scoring *how clean* their traffic is. A
TrustScore/Fraud service is one of the future event consumers in
[[content-and-scale-strategy]].
