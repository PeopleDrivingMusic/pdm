# Artist Grades (anti-spam gating)

An artist cannot launch crowdfunding immediately. They progress through stages that
act as an anti-spam / quality filter before financial mechanics unlock:

1. **Newbie** — 1 track, basic page.
2. **Active** — 100+ subscribers, regular posts for 30 days.
3. **Creator** — verified activity and healthy [[trustscore]] → access to
   fundraising ([[revenue-share-crowdfunding]]).

Grades gate **capability** (what an artist may do); [[trustscore]] scores
**traffic cleanliness**. Together they protect the [[economic-model]] from abuse and
support the cautious sequencing in [[defer-investment-layer]].
