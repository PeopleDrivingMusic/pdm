# Marketing

Home for analytical data, marketing campaigns, and growth plans. Use it to store
market/competitor analytics and to build campaigns and launch plans on top of the
product/strategy knowledge in this wiki.

Conventions:
- `type: marketing`, one topic per page, `kebab-case.md`.
- Ground campaigns in [[positioning]] (category bet) and [[economic-model]]
  (monetization), and target the wedge audiences in [[positioning]] (Target wedge).
- Separate **evergreen analysis** (market, competitors, ICP) from **time-bound
  campaigns** (date them in frontmatter / title).

Suggested first pages when data arrives: `competitors.md` (Spotify/Patreon/Bandcamp/
audius positioning), `target-segments.md`, `launch-plan.md`, `channels.md`.
