# PDM (Producer Direct Model) — Concept Document v1.0

## 1. General project overview
**PDM** is a next-generation music ecosystem that объединites a streaming platform, a direct funding tool for artists, and a copyright exchange.

**Mission:** Return доходs to real artists by giving fans the ability to become micro-investors and co-owners of music assets.

**Key shift:** Move from “pay per number of plays” to “build and monetize a loyal fan base.”

---

## 2. Economic model
### 2.1. Subscription model (Artist-Centric)
- The user does not buy a general platform subscription.
- The user subscribes to a specific artist for **$1/month**.
- **Split:** 80% to the artist, 20% to the platform.
- **Listening revenue:** The pool from subscriptions is distributed among tracks in proportion to listening time by a specific user (User-Centric Model).

### 2.2. Crowdfunding and Investments (Revenue Share)
- An artist can offer a share (e.g., 20%) of future royalties for a track via crowdfunding.
- Fans buy these shares with fiat/crypto.
- Shareholders receive a percentage of the track’s revenue not only inside PDM, but also from external platforms (Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube), where PDM acts as the aggregator-licensee.

### 2.3. Financial logic
- **In:** Cards/Cryptocurrency.
- **Out:** Cryptocurrency only (or an internal PDM token), enabling global payouts without cross-border delays.

---

## 3. Functional capabilities (Features)

### 3.1. For Fans (Investors)
1. **Exclusive content:** Access to private posts, videos, demos, and artist polls.
2. **Commenting:** Available only to paid subscribers (spam protection).
3. **Offline Mode:** Caching tracks of the subscribed artist.
4. **Presales:** Priority access to ticket and merch purchases.
5. **No Ads:** Complete absence of ads in tracks of the paid artist.
6. **Loyalty tiers:** Spending from $5/month removes ads across the entire app. From $10/month — global discounts on merch and events.
7. **Producer cabinet:** Interface to track returns from owned shares in tracks and artist growth.

### 3.2. For Artists
1. **Direct income:** Receive subscription funds immediately.
2. **Launchpad:** Ability to legally raise a budget for a video/album by selling shares in copyrights.
3. **Data analytics:** Heat map of paying fans for tour planning.
4. **Protection from AI:** Identity and engagement verification system.

---

## 4. TrustScore system and bot protection
To prevent manipulation by AI farms, a two-sided reputation system is introduced:

- **User TrustScore:** Evaluates listener behavior patterns. Bots have minimal “weight,” their streams barely affect money distribution.
- **Artist TrustScore:** Evaluates traffic cleanliness. Manipulation reduces crowdfunding limits and disables recommendations.
- **Goal:** Make maintaining bot farms economically unprofitable.

---

## 5. Workflow (User journey)

1. **Registration:** User enters PDM, listens to music for free (with limitations).
2. **Subscription:** Chooses an artist, pays $1. Caching and chat are unlocked.
3. **Investment:** Artist opens a raise for a new track. User buys 0.1% share for $5.
4. **Ownership:** Track releases. Revenue from PDM and external streaming accumulates in the system.
5. **Payout:** User sees income in the “Producer cabinet” and withdraws in crypto.

---

## 6. Artist grades (Anti-spam filter)
An artist cannot launch crowdfunding immediately. They must pass stages:
1. **Newbie:** 1 track, basic page.
2. **Active:** 100+ subscribers, regular posts (30 days).
3. **Creator:** Verified activity and TrustScore -> Access to fundraising.

---

## 7. PDM’s role in the industry
PDM does not compete with major labels, it acts as an **incubator**.
- The platform proves the artist’s market fit.
- When an artist moves to a major label, fans can get an “Exit” (their shares bought out by the label at a multiplier).
